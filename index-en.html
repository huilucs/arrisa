A practice rooted in presence,
shaped by curiosity.

I'm Arissa — a yoga teacher, sound healer, and aerial practitioner based in Taiwan. My path winds through mat practice, singing bowls, silk hammocks, and the ocean — all of them ways of returning to the body and the breath.

I believe a good practice is generous: it has room for the child taking her first tree pose, for the elder finding new range in her hips, for the busy professional unwinding after work. My classes are calm, embodied, and approachable — never performative.

What I teach · 課程介紹

Three doorways into the same stillness.

Whether you arrive on a mat, in a silk hammock, or lying beneath the resonance of a singing bowl, the destination is the same — a quieter body, a clearer mind.

Mat Yoga

墊 上 瑜 伽

Hatha and vinyasa flows that build strength gently and unwind tight shoulders, hips, and lower backs.

  • Beginner-friendly group classes
  • Restorative & gentle yin
  • Multi-generational classes (kids → seniors)
  • Private 1:1 sessions

Sound Healing

頌 缽 療 癒

A guided journey with Tibetan singing bowls, gongs, and chimes. Lie back, close your eyes, and let resonance do the work.

  • Group sound baths (60–75 min)
  • Yoga + sound combo sessions
  • 1:1 sound healing for stress & sleep
  • Workshops for schools & companies

Aerial Yoga

空 中 瑜 伽

Suspended in soft silks, the spine decompresses, the core engages, and inversions become accessible — and joyful.

  • Beginner aerial fundamentals
  • Aerial flow & strength
  • Restorative aerial / hammock rest
  • Small-group classes (max 8)
